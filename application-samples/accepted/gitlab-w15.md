# YC Application — GitLab (W15)

## Company

**Company name:**
GitLab

**Company URL, if any:**
https://about.gitlab.com/

**If you have an online demo, what's the URL?**
http://demo.gitlab.com/users/sign_in
(Note from author: this URL no longer works; in 2016 the sign-up URL is https://gitlab.com/users/sign_in.)

**What is your company going to make?**
We're making open source software to collaborate on code. It started as "run your own GitHub" that most users deploy on their own server(s).

## Founders

**Please tell us about an interesting project, preferably outside of class or work, that two or more of you created together.**
We created GitLab together and now over 100,000 organizations are using it. We also created GitLab CI and GitLab CI Runner together.

**How long have the founders known one another and how did you meet?**
In 2011 Dmitriy started GitLab. We met in 2012 via email when Sytse started building GitLab.com. In 2013 we formally started a company together.

## Progress

**How far along are you?**
Over 100,000 organizations are using GitLab. <Redacted>, Qualcomm, NASA, Nasdaq OMX and Interpol are paying customers.

**If you've already started working on it, how long have you been working?**
Since 2011, over 10,000 commits.

**Which of the following best describes your progress?**
Launched.

**When will you have a prototype or beta? How many users do you have?**
We estimate more than 1M.

**Do you have revenue?**
Yes.

**How much revenue?**
$1M annual revenue run rate.

**What is your monthly growth rate?**
About 60% in revenue each month.

## Idea

**Why did you pick this idea to work on?**
Dmitriy wanted a solution he could use at his previous job. All employees except our account managers are software developers.

**What's new about what you're making?**
We offer a better way of collaborating on digital products (the feature branch workflow) to organizations that prefer to work on open source tools.

**Who are your competitors?**
GitHub Enterprise and Atlassian Stash are our primary competitors. We fear Atlassian Stash most since the GitHub Enterprise offering is weak.

**What do you understand about your business that other companies in it just don't get?**
An open source development process allows you to market your product for free. The development is also more efficient because the community contributes a lot of features. We believe that version control is infrastructure software and that open source is the natural model for that, much like how Linux is the dominant model for server operating systems.

**How do or will you make money?**
Mostly by selling subscriptions that entitle our customers to support and our proprietary GitLab Enterprise Edition.

**How will you get users?**
Currently we get users through word of mouth (amplified by Twitter).

## Other

**Please tell us something surprising or amusing that one of you has discovered.**
Before GitLab, Sytse built recreational manned submarines from scratch; the company he started is currently the largest producer of them in the world and is called U-Boat Worx.
